About Us

InAmigos Foundation was founded on 23 September 2020 by Mr. Govind Shukla. It is a Section 8 registered non-profit organization dedicated to creating lasting social impact across India through education, sustainability, women empowerment, animal welfare and skill development initiatives.

Recognitions

  • Section 8 Registered
  • 80G & 12A Certified
  • CSR-1 Registered
  • NITI Aayog Registered
  • ISO 9001:2015 Certified
